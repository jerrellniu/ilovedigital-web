## The challenge

Evolved Solar and Electrical install solar, batteries and electrical work across the Gold Coast and Northern Rivers. Steven, the director, is a licensed Master Electrician and an accredited solar installer, and the business does its own electrical work rather than subcontracting it.

None of that was showing up in search.

At the February 2026 benchmark the site drew 24 clicks and 847 impressions in 28 days, from 96 unique queries. Around 90 per cent of clicks came from people already typing the business name. Non-brand searches produced impressions and almost nothing else.

Underneath that were structural problems. The site carried no structured data. The schema that did exist described a different business entirely, a similarly named operator in northern New South Wales. Seven pages returned 404s. Blog posts were intercepting commercial queries that service pages should have owned. The phone number on the site did not match the Google Business Profile.

## The approach

Fix the foundations before chasing rankings. Tell Google exactly what this business is, where it works and who stands behind the advice, then build the pages that answer what people are actually searching for.

## The work

**Technical repair.** Seven 404s redirected, duplicate redirects cleaned, and two page-with-redirect errors corrected in Search Console. The homepage hero video replaced with drone footage, then sharpened again through a Vimeo embed. Service page hero contrast fixed.

**Schema, done properly.** A single validated graph across the site — Organization plus LocalBusiness and Electrician, linked by ID, with opening hours, price range and service-area suburbs. Person markup naming Steven as the author of the site's content. FAQPage on the FAQs, hybrid and battery pages. Article markup on every blog post. The wrong business entity removed, and the trading name standardised everywhere.

**Content.** The FAQs page expanded from eight questions to twenty, written to be quotable by AI search as well as readable by people. Author attribution corrected to Steven across all articles. Expanded copy for the hybrid and battery and switchboard upgrade pages. A Burleigh Heads suburb page. A Queensland solar battery rebate article.

**Project pages.** Five real installs published at /projects — Currumbin Waters, Loganholme, Molendinar, Mudgeeraba and Pimpama — with 28 photos resized, optimised and given descriptive filenames and alt text, lazy loading, and a privacy review of every image before publication.

**Finance page rebuilt.** The green loans page was rewritten to carry verified rates, fees, eligibility and comparison rates, an FAQ with schema, and a credit disclaimer it had never had. Every lender product was re-verified against the lender's own page on the day of publication, and an unevidenced savings claim was removed.

**Google Business Profile.** Categories expanded beyond solar to cover electrician, electrical installation, equipment supply and EV charging, and reviews answered.

## The results

All figures from Google Search Console unless stated.

**February 2026 benchmark against the period to 8 May 2026.**

- Impressions rose from 847 to 11,914, an increase of 1,307 per cent
- Unique ranking queries rose from 96 to 595, an increase of 520 per cent
- Clicks rose from 24 in 28 days to 103
- Fifteen or more queries sitting on or near page one

**5 July to 1 August 2026.** Eighty-one clicks from 10,551 impressions at 0.77 per cent click-through, average position 20.3. Australian traffic accounted for 95 per cent of clicks. The battery article ran at 2.48 per cent click-through, roughly three times the site average.

**Local search, 4 August 2026.** Fourth in organic results and sixth in the local finder for solar installer Burleigh Heads, ahead of two competitors carrying 821 and 171 reviews respectively. The Google Business Profile sits at 5.0 across 33 reviews.

Broad terms like solar Gold Coast remain a long way back, around position 45 in the local finder. That is the next piece of work, not a solved problem.

## What runs now

Monthly search work, content production, Google Business Profile management and a monthly review with the numbers on the table.

## FAQ

**Can you rank a small installer against the national brands?**

Not on the broad terms, not quickly. On suburb and product terms, yes. This site now outranks operators with twenty times the review count on the searches that happen closest to a job.

**Does schema markup actually matter?**

It is how a search engine knows what your business is rather than guessing. In this case the site was publishing data for the wrong company entirely, which no amount of content would have fixed.

**Do you need a new website first?**

Not always. This one stayed on Squarespace. The work was rebuilding the pages that mattered, adding the structure underneath and giving the site something worth ranking.
