## The problem

A colour-matching card game made in Queensland. The store was on Wix and it was not selling the way it should. Around half the people who landed on the home page never made it to the product, and a launch into the US produced no US sales at all. The owner needed a store built to sell, in time for a run of advertising and game events.

## What we did

- Moved the store off Wix and onto Shopify
- Rebuilt the home page and product page around getting people to buy
- Gave the brand a proper look, built from the game's own colours
- Kept working on it afterwards — clearer answers to buyer questions, and search work so people find the game on Google

## The result

The owner set herself a sales goal for the first six months. She hit it in a few weeks.

The store now carries 81 customer reviews at four and a half stars, and we set it up so that rating shows next to the listing in Google — proof doing its job at the moment someone is deciding.
