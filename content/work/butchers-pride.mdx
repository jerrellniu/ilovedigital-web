## The problem

Butchers Pride is a range of small-batch cooking sauces made by chef Phil Strasser. In December 2025 the brand had no social presence at all — no audience, no content, no system for making any. The Google listing had been suspended and was invisible, and what it did show was out of date.

## What we did

- Built a monthly content plan and ran it — carousels, Reels, recipes, stockist posts, event coverage
- Wrote every caption in Phil's voice
- Got the Google listing reinstated and corrected
- Reported every month on what worked and what did not

## The result

From nothing to 1,482 followers in five months, with engagement running at 4.7 per cent — a strong rate for an account that size — and the Google listing visible again.

When the numbers showed that social on its own would not carry the brand, we said so rather than keep selling posts. Phil kept everything: the brand guidelines, the content system and the calendars, handed over in full.
