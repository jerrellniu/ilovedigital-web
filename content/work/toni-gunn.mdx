## The problem

Toni is a counsellor, clinical supervisor and family therapist. Most of her work comes through referrals, so the website is not there to win business — it is there to hold up when someone looks her up before booking.

The old WordPress site was doing the opposite. It was several years out of date, awkward for anyone to change, and costing her a monthly maintenance fee plus developer time whenever something needed fixing.

## What we did

- Moved her off WordPress and onto Squarespace, so the site maintains itself
- Kept everything on one address rather than splitting supervision and workshops apart
- Built a simple three-way split so a visitor picks their path in one click: supervision, workshops, counselling
- Wrote the content in plain language, broken into pieces people actually read, with the jargon taken out
- Left booking software out on purpose, so enquiries come to her and she decides who is a fit
- Put her professional registrations where they can be seen, and steered the photography away from AI images to a real shoot

## The result

One site, on her own domain, that says what she does and reads like her.

The monthly maintenance bill is gone, and so is the wait for a developer every time a date or a price changes. For a practice run two days a week alongside teaching, that is the point.
