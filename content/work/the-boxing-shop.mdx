## The problem

The Boxing Shop is a boxing gym and equipment retailer in Salisbury, Brisbane, running three sides of one business: the shop, the gym, and Boxing Beyond Barriers, their community program for disengaged kids.

All three lived on separate websites. The main address showed a "coming soon" page. The online store had no traffic and no sales. The charity site sat on its own platform, dated and disconnected. Anyone searching for boxing gear or a gym in Brisbane found none of it.

## What we did

- Consolidated all three sites into one Shopify store covering the shop, the gym and the charity
- Pointed the real domain at it and retired the coming soon page
- Kept their existing class booking system and built it into the new site rather than replacing it
- Rebuilt the home page around who actually walks in — juniors first, then adults, women's and personal training
- Wrote titles, descriptions and category copy for every page and collection, so Google had something to read
- Redirected 193 old addresses to the new ones, then shut the old sites down

## The result

Organic clicks rose 31 per cent in a month, and Google flagged the site passing 500 clicks in 28 days.

They now sit first for their own name, and their community program comes up first and second when AI search engines are asked about boxing programs for disengaged youth in Brisbane.

Three platforms became one, six weeks from kickoff to launch.
