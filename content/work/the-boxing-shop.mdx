## The problem

Gareth Williams coaches boxers, sells boxing gear and runs a youth charity out of The Boxing Shop in Salisbury, Brisbane. He also had three websites. A WordPress site for his classes that nobody had touched in years. A separate site for Boxing Beyond Barriers, his program that takes boxing into schools. And a Shopify store with no sales.

Gareth was paying for three platforms and getting nothing back from any of them. Booking one of his classes was hard. Buying his gear was harder. His schools program had nowhere to send people.

The job was not a new website. It was fewer of them.

## What we did

- Closed Gareth's WordPress sites and moved everything to one Shopify store
- Built a custom home page around his classes, with bookings through the system he already ran
- Built a branded page for his youth programs so schools and parents have one place to look
- Set the store up to sell his equipment properly, with the pages Google needs to find it
- Redirected 193 old addresses so nothing was lost, then shut the old sites down

## The result

Gareth's running costs are down 60 per cent. Traffic is up 17 per cent month on month. And his class bookings now come in online, in numbers the old sites never produced.

Three platforms became one, six weeks from kickoff to launch.
